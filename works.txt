1999–2004: Early career
Year	Comic series	Significance
1999–2000	Stars and S.T.R.I.P.E.	His first major DC series, introducing Courtney Whitmore, later known as Stargirl.
2000–2006	JSA	His defining early run on the Justice Society, initially co-written with David S. Goyer.
2000–2005	The Flash	His acclaimed Wally West run, expanding the mythology of the Rogues.
2002–2003	Hawkman	Revitalized Hawkman following the JSA stories.
2002–2004	The Avengers (Marvel)	Wrote issues #57–76, including World Trust and Red Zone.
2002–2003	The Vision (Marvel)	Four-issue miniseries exploring the Vision's identity.
2003–2007	Teen Titans	Relaunched the team with characters including Superboy, Robin, Wonder Girl, and Kid Flash.

Sources: DC Comics, Marvel Comics, and Geoff Johns' bibliography.

2004–2011: Green Lantern and major DC events
Year	Comic series or event	Significance
2004–2005	Green Lantern: Rebirth	Restored Hal Jordan as Green Lantern.
2005–2013	Green Lantern	Long-running cosmic saga introducing the emotional spectrum and expanding the Lantern Corps.
2005–2006	Infinite Crisis	Major DC crossover revisiting the legacy of Crisis on Infinite Earths.
2006–2007	52	Weekly series co-written with Grant Morrison, Greg Rucka, and Mark Waid.
2006–2009	Action Comics	Superman stories including Last Son, Escape from Bizarro World, and Brainiac.
2007–2009	Justice Society of America	New JSA series, including Thy Kingdom Come.
2007–2008	Booster Gold	Time-travel adventures co-written with Jeff Katz.
2007–2008	Sinestro Corps War	Major Green Lantern crossover within his ongoing run.
2008–2009	Final Crisis: Legion of Three Worlds	Story featuring Superman, the Legion of Super-Heroes, and Superboy-Prime.
2009–2010	The Flash: Rebirth	Restored Barry Allen to a central role in DC Comics.
2009–2010	Superman: Secret Origin	Retelling of Superman's early life and origin.
2009–2010	Blackest Night	Universe-wide Green Lantern event involving the Black Lantern Corps.
2010–2011	Brightest Day	Follow-up series exploring the aftermath of Blackest Night.
2010–2011	The Flash	A new ongoing series starring Barry Allen.
2011	Flashpoint	Reality-altering event that led into DC's New 52 relaunch.

Sources: DC Comics' creator profile and reading collection.

2011–2020: The New 52, Rebirth, and Watchmen
Year	Comic series or event	Significance
2011–2016	Justice League	His major New 52 run, beginning with Origin and concluding with Darkseid War.
2011–2013	Aquaman	Reimagined Aquaman with stories including The Trench and Throne of Atlantis.
2012–2021	Batman: Earth One	Three graphic novels with Gary Frank, reinterpreting Batman's origins.
2012–2013	Shazam!	Modernized Billy Batson's origin in backup stories published in Justice League.
2013	Justice League of America	Introduced a government-backed superhero team during the New 52 era.
2013–2014	Forever Evil	DC crossover placing the villains at the center of the story.
2014–2015	Superman: The Men of Tomorrow	Superman storyline illustrated by John Romita Jr.
2015–2016	Justice League: Darkseid War	Climactic storyline of his Justice League run.
2016	DC Universe: Rebirth #1	Established the central mystery of DC Rebirth.
2017–2019	Doomsday Clock	Twelve-issue crossover connecting the DC Universe and Watchmen.
2018–2020	Shazam!	New ongoing comic exploring Billy Batson and the Magiclands.
2020	Batman: Three Jokers	Three-issue Batman story illustrated by Jason Fabok.
